# Use a human-anchored semantic baseline for approved complex patterns

The frozen candidate-independent Umbrella patterned-v2 result remains a factual
FAIL, but it cannot express the product decision that the current analytic
flower, leaf, and branch program is an acceptable semantic alternative to the
Authored Reference. Patterned-v3 therefore names the human-approved candidate
as a positive exemplar, makes exact-position DeltaE, SSIM, palette clustering,
and v2 recall diagnostic, and gates position-tolerant motif-family coverage,
role palette, unchanged material, unchanged geometry, and destructive controls.

This is explicitly candidate-informed evidence, not a Quarantined Rebaseline or
a retroactive v2 pass. The positive candidate is hash-frozen; flat, wrong-role
palette, deleted flower/leaf/branch, and half-coverage variants must remain
rejected. A changed candidate requires a new positive-exemplar version.
