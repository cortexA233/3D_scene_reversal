# Single Mesh Lab Stage 1.5 v3 and eight-object Stage 2

Status: active

## Problem Statement

Stage 1 remains a historical `2/4 FAIL`, while the subsequent versioned Stone
recovery passes and the user has explicitly approved the current Umbrella
patterned appearance. The candidate-independent patterned-v2 metrics cannot
represent that product decision without accepting destructive candidates. Four
Lab objects remain unimplemented, and the earlier six-object exit no longer
matches the required 8/8 integrated delivery.

## Solution

Stage 1.5 closes by freezing a human-anchored Umbrella patterned-v3 baseline,
proving that the approved positive passes while six semantic damage controls
fail, retaining every geometry/nonvisual threshold, and completing two stable
native hardware-GPU runs in Firefox and Safari. It then pre-calibrates separate
category baselines and compactness budgets for Bamboo Shoot, Mushroom, Blue Hat,
and Candle before fitting any of those candidates.

Stage 2 implements the four remaining objects sequentially in their fixed Lab
slots. Each object must pass its frozen visual baseline, nonvisual ceilings,
determinism, Reference Independence, Chrome acceptance, and native Firefox and
Safari gates before the next object begins. Final certification requires 8/8.

## Fixed Contracts

- Historical Stage 1 and patterned-v2 reports and thresholds never change.
- Patterned-v3 is explicitly candidate-informed and names the hash-frozen,
  human-approved Umbrella as a positive exemplar.
- Exact-position complex-texture DeltaE, SSIM, palette clustering, and v2 recall
  are diagnostic in v3; bounded motif-family coverage and role palette are hard.
- Existing Umbrella geometry and all nonvisual thresholds remain unchanged.
- Stage 2 category-v1 thresholds and budgets freeze before candidate fitting.
  A later user-authorized relaxation never edits v1 and must be a separately
  versioned Human-anchored baseline with a hash-frozen positive and retained
  rejection of every declared destructive control.
- Final delivery is one eight-object Reference-layout Delivery, never per-object
  final scenes and never an island-village source-world arrangement.
- Runtime textures, sampled appearance/geometry, vectorized bitmaps, WASM, and
  unapproved Runtime Kernels remain prohibited.

## Acceptance

- Umbrella v3 passes Chrome, Firefox, and Safari and rejects all six damage controls.
- Four remaining object baselines and budgets are frozen before fitting.
- Bamboo Shoot, Mushroom, Blue Hat, and Candle each pass their own full gates.
- The integrated scene contains all eight Procedural Replacements in the fixed
  Eight-slot Lab Reference Layout.
- The final report says `PASS under versioned category-specific baselines (8/8)`
  and retains Stage 1 as a separate `2/4 FAIL` historical result.

## Stage 2 Progress

- Bamboo Shoot: accepted under semantic appearance v2.
- Mushroom: accepted under compact geometry v2 and semantic appearance v2.
- Blue Hat: accepted under unchanged category-v1 geometry and semantic panel-
  pattern appearance v2; exact-position complex-texture metrics remain
  diagnostic and all four declared role-damage controls reject.
- Candle: in progress; the eight-object exit is not yet claimed.

## Out of Scope

- full-island reconstruction or complete-island tickets;
- terrain, village structures, scatter, wildlife, interaction, animation, or LOD;
- rewriting v1/v2 evidence, silently lowering a frozen baseline, or lowering
  nonvisual limits after fitting;
- isolated per-object final delivery routes.
