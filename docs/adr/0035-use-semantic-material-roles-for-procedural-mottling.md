# Use semantic material roles for procedural mottling

Bamboo Shoot passes every frozen Stage 2 category-v1 geometry metric, but its
resolution-independent mottling cannot reproduce the Authored Reference's
texels at the same image positions. The category-v1 appearance FAIL remains
historical evidence. A separately versioned semantic appearance v2 keeps the
unchanged category-v1 geometry gate and makes exact-position DeltaE, SSIM, and
palette clustering diagnostic while hard-gating bounded brown-core and green-
sheath coverage, role distance, roughness, and metalness.

The v2 positive is object-source hash-frozen. Deleting the sheath role, rotating
the role palette, or collapsing the object to one flat role must reject while
geometry remains passing. This boundary may be reused for other genuinely
complex procedural appearances only with object-specific roles and destructive
controls; it is not a general texture discount and never rewrites category v1.
