# Stop Stage 1.5 at the Umbrella pattern boundary

The geometry-frozen Umbrella Bounded Semantic Pattern Program is a valid
negative experiment. It preserves the frozen two-batch, 5,336-triangle
geometry and semantic hierarchy, passes the complete-source 83/96 scalar audit,
all unchanged nonvisual budgets, determinism, and Reference Independence, and
contains no texture, pixel table, sampled grid, lookup image, vectorized bitmap,
or resolution-scaled appearance payload.

Under the candidate-independent `patterned-appearance-baseline-v2`, the
normative Chrome run fails all seven appearance hard metrics. Mean and P90
DeltaE are 10.1431 and 56.9890; mean and worst-view SSIM are 0.4189 and 0.2800;
flower, leaf, and branch Semantic Pattern Recall are 0.0157, 0.0443, and 0.0100.
The corresponding frozen limits remain 6.6966, 30.8300, 0.7287, 0.5870,
0.0451, 0.1615, and 0.5905. Candidate evidence cannot alter them.

The sequential Stage 1.5 kill condition therefore stops the effort before
native Firefox/Safari candidate runs, Bamboo Shoot/Mushroom pre-calibration, or
final Stage 1.5 certification. Stage 2 is not authorized, the historical Stage
1 result remains 2/4 FAIL, and no partial or retroactive Versioned Category Exit
is claimed. A future specification must explicitly revisit the hero-pattern
representation budget or the full-island Code-only Production Runtime target;
it may not continue by lowering this frozen baseline.
