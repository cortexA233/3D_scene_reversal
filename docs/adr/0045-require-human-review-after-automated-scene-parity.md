# Require human review after automated scene parity

Final full-island parity requires both the automated Scene Parity Gate Stack and a subsequent Human Parity Review of the six-camera comparisons, difference evidence, and authored overview composition. Human judgment cannot waive structural, geometry, or appearance failures, but an obvious residual mismatch after automated passage identifies a metric blind spot that must be stated concretely and converted into reference-only calibrated evidence or a versioned gate revision; this preserves perceptual accountability without permitting unexplained taste overrides or candidate-driven threshold relaxation.
