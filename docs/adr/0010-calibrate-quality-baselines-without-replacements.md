# Calibrate quality baselines without replacements

Quality Baselines are calibrated once from repeated Authored Reference renders and controlled perturbations to scale, pivot, rotation, topology, profile resolution, and color before Stone Path replacement fitting begins. Initial numeric gates are declared in the experiment specification, may be corrected once using that sensitivity evidence, and are then frozen for the tranche. A failed Procedural Replacement cannot silently relax its gate; changing a baseline later is an explicit experimental finding that requires re-baselining every affected object.
