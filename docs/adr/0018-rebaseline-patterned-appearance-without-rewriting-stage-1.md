# Rebaseline patterned appearance without rewriting Stage 1

The frozen `single-mesh-quality-baseline-v1` and its two-of-four Stage 1 result remain unchanged historical evidence. Before fitting a new Umbrella appearance candidate, Stage 1.5 will calibrate a separately versioned Patterned Appearance Baseline v2 from Authored Reference perturbations; its appearance tolerances may be materially looser than v1, but a flat canopy, wrong dominant palette, deleted major motif family, materially reduced pattern coverage, and large phase or pattern-scale errors must still fail, and no further loosening is permitted after candidate fitting begins. Umbrella's geometry gates and nonvisual budgets remain unchanged, and any later pass must be reported as a rebaselined Stage 1.5 result rather than a retroactive Stage 1 pass.
