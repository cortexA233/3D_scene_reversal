# Bound Stone's support polyhedron to 24 directions

Stone's sole Stage 1.5 geometry candidate intersects at most 24 fixed canonical-direction support planes, with only compact whole-form orientation, seeded-deformation, and existing appearance controls inside the unchanged 32-scalar budget. The source is a credible convex target—its measured convex-hull excess is about 0.0032% by volume and 0.00035% by area—but the recipe may not copy arbitrary source face normals or expand the direction set in response to fitting; failure of this bounded representation against the complete v1 gates is the second reasonable compact-representation failure and triggers boundary review.
